How it works

From stranger to circle in 4 steps

1

Sign up

Create your free account and tell us what you're into. Takes 2 minutes.

2

Discover communities

Browse vetted circles filtered by category, vibe, and who they're for.

3

Join & connect

Hit join to get into the WhatsApp group and start meeting people.

4

Show up IRL

RSVP to events, attend meetups, and build friendships that last.

There's no wrong way to find your circle.

Some people find their people on the very first event. Others take a few tries — and that's completely fine.

Every event you show up to builds something. Confidence. Familiarity. A face you recognise across the room. The anxiety gets smaller each time. The connections get deeper.

If you've found your circle — beautiful.
If you're still exploring — that's the whole point.

Circles isn't about finding your people fast.
It's about finding the right ones.
